How this is computed
Moments are bucketed into 48 half-hour bins by local timestamp. Each petal's colour is the dominant emotion in that bin; its radial length is √(count), scaled against the busiest bin. Sundowning is reported for 15:00–19:00, a clinically observed time of restlessness in dementia care.
Khachiyants, Trinkle, Son & Kim (2011). Sundown syndrome in persons with dementia. Psychiatry Investigation. · Compatible with actigraphy / HRV time-series data sources.
