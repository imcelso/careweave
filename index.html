What care staff notice

CareWeave is a working prototype for care documentation in a residential care home. It shows how staff record everyday care moments, read them alongside routines and earlier notes, and prepare shift handover. AI suggests a reading or drafts the note; staff edit, correct or discard both. The interface uses interactive timelines and annotated charts to show how care patterns change over time without reducing them to scores.

How the prototype works
1

Record a moment. Staff choose a resident, note the emotional tone and add a short observation.

2

Read it in context. The entry appears alongside routines, preferences and notes from earlier shifts.

3

Prepare handover. Selected moments form a draft handover note that staff edit and confirm.

Research prototype. The interface uses pre-set content rather than a live model. No data is collected and all residents are fictional. Runs in a browser on desktop or tablet.
